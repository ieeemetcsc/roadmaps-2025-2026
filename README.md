## 📖 About IEEE MET CS Chapter

The **IEEE MET Computer Society Chapter** is a student-led community at the Faculty of Engineering, Mataria (MET), dedicated to empowering students with practical technical skills and industry-relevant knowledge.

### Who We Are
We are a vibrant group of students, learners, and tech enthusiasts committed to bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world application. As part of the global IEEE Computer Society, we uphold the values of innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning.

### Our Mission
Our mission is to:
- Provide structured, beginner-friendly learning paths for students at all levels
- Foster a culture of hands-on learning through projects, workshops, and challenges
- Build a supportive community where members can grow together
- Equip students with the technical and soft skills needed to excel in the tech industry

### Our Focus
We believe that the best way to learn is by doing. That's why our roadmaps emphasize **practical projects**, **real-world tools**, and **step-by-step progression** — ensuring that every student, regardless of their background, can build confidence and competence in their chosen field.

## 🌟 What is This Repository?

This repository is the official home of the **IEEE MET CS Chapter Learning Roadmaps** for the 2025–2026 season.

### Why These Roadmaps Exist
Many students struggle with where to start, what to learn next, or how to organize their learning journey. The tech landscape can feel overwhelming, especially for beginners. These roadmaps solve that problem by providing:
- **Clear, structured paths** from beginner to advanced
- **Curated resources** including courses, articles, tools, and tutorials
- **Milestone-based progression** to track your growth
- **Practical project ideas** to apply what you learn

### How They Help Students
Our roadmaps guide you through:
1. **Fundamentals**: Building a strong foundation with core concepts
2. **Tools & Technologies**: Learning industry-standard tools and frameworks
3. **Practice & Projects**: Applying knowledge through hands-on work
4. **Advanced Topics**: Exploring deeper concepts and specializations

### Our Philosophy
We believe in learning by doing. Each roadmap follows a **step-by-step approach**, starting from zero knowledge and progressively building up to industry-level competence. You'll move from understanding basics to building real projects — because the best way to learn is to build.

## 📂 Repository Structure

This repository is organized into **7 main tracks**, each containing a detailed roadmap:

### 🤖 **ai-ml**
Explore the world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Learn data science fundamentals, machine learning algorithms, deep learning, neural networks, and modern AI frameworks. Perfect for those interested in building intelligent systems.

### ⚙️ **backend**
Master server-side development with comprehensive roadmaps for different backend technologies. Learn about databases, APIs, authentication, deployment, and scalable architecture. Contains specialized paths for Node.js and .NET development.

### 🎨 **frontend**
Dive into front-end web development. Learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, modern frameworks (React, Vue, Angular), responsive design, and best practices for building interactive user interfaces.

### 📱 **flutter**
Build beautiful cross-platform mobile applications with Flutter. Learn Dart programming, Flutter widgets, state management, API integration, and deployment for both iOS and Android.

### 🔐 **cybersecurity**
Enter the world of information security. Learn about network security, ethical hacking, cryptography, penetration testing, security tools, and how to protect systems from threats.

### 🎭 **ui-ux**
Master the art and science of user experience design. Learn design thinking, wireframing, prototyping, user research, accessibility, and tools like Figma and Adobe XD to create delightful user experiences.

### 🔌 **hardware**
Explore embedded systems and hardware development. Learn about microcontrollers, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, IoT, circuit design, and how to bring physical computing projects to life.

## 🗺️ How to Use These Roadmaps

### Step 1: Choose Your Track
Browse the folders above and select the track that interests you most. Don't worry if you're not sure — you can always explore multiple tracks or switch later!

### Step 2: Read the Roadmap
Each track folder contains a detailed README or roadmap file. Open it and read through:
- The learning path structure
- Prerequisites (if any)
- Resources and materials
- Project suggestions

### Step 3: Follow the Path
Start from the beginning if you're a complete beginner, or jump to the section that matches your current level. The roadmaps are designed to be flexible.

### Step 4: Practice & Build
- Work through the suggested resources
- Complete hands-on exercises
- Build the recommended projects
- Share your progress with the community

### Step 5: Stay Consistent
Learning is a marathon, not a sprint. Set realistic goals, track your progress, and don't hesitate to ask for help in our community channels.

### 💡 Our Recommendations:
- **Take notes** as you learn
- **Build projects** to solidify your understanding
- **Join study groups** with fellow members
- **Participate in workshops** and events
- **Contribute back** by improving these roadmaps
