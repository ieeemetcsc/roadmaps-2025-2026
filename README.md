# IEEE MET Computer Society  
## Learning Roadmaps — 2025–2026

This repository contains structured learning roadmaps created by the **IEEE MET Computer Society Chapter** for the **2025–2026 season**.  
The roadmaps are designed to guide students and self-learners from fundamentals to practical, real-world and industry-relevant skills.

---

## About the Chapter

The **IEEE MET Computer Society Chapter** is a student organization at  
**Misr Higher Institute of Engineering and Technology (M.E.T), Mansoura**.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic study and industry needs through:
- Practical learning
- Project-based experience
- Strong technical foundations
- A collaborative learning community

---

## Repository Overview

Each track folder contains its own roadmap, learning resources, and suggested practice paths.

### Available Tracks

-  [AI & Machine Learning](./ai-ml)

## Who This Is For

- University students and IEEE members  
- Beginners with no prior experience  
- Self-learners and career switchers
